Tips to Reduce Your Carbon Footprint

Smart Irrigation

Switch to drip irrigation to reduce water usage by up to 60% and lower energy costs associated with pumping.

Organic Fertilizers

Use organic compost and biofertilizers to reduce chemical fertilizer usage and improve soil health naturally.

Renewable Energy

Install solar panels to power irrigation pumps and farm equipment, reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

Integrated Pest Management

Adopt IPM practices to reduce pesticide usage while effectively managing pests through biological controls.

Agroforestry

Plant trees on your farm to sequester carbon, provide shade, and create additional income streams.

Efficient Machinery

Maintain farm equipment regularly and consider upgrading to more fuel-efficient models.
